The short version

  • You never create an account. We do not ask for or collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone, not on our servers. We do not run any servers that receive your data.
  • The free games show ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • Optional purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data and we use no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

1. Game progress stored on your device

Your progress (heroes, coins, gems, trophies, settings and the list of items you bought) is kept in the app's private storage on your phone so the game works offline. If you have turned on Google backup on your phone, Android may include this save in your encrypted Google account backup so it comes back on a new phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android Settings deletes the save from the phone.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games show rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to double a reward) and, between matches, an occasional full-screen ad. Players who buy anything, or who buy Remove Ads, do not see full-screen ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects the following and shares it with Google:

  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which Google uses to estimate an approximate location such as your country;
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, video completions);
  • device and app information (phone model, Android version, language) and diagnostics such as crash logs of the ads SDK.

On supported phones the SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising features (ad topics and ad measurement), which you can manage in Android Settings under Privacy, Ads.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings, Privacy options. Without consent you may still see non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings under Privacy, Ads.

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (gem packs, a starter pack, Remove Ads, the season pass and extra heroes) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. The game receives from Google Play only what it needs to deliver and restore your item: the product ID, an order ID, a purchase token and the purchase state. Refunds and payment questions go through Google Play.

4. Leaderboards (Google Play Games, only if switched on)

Online leaderboards are not switched on yet. If a future update adds them and you choose to use them, your Play Games profile name, avatar and scores will be shared with Google Play Games Services so other players can see them. Google's Privacy Policy covers that data, and you can manage your profile's visibility in the Play Games app.

5. Permissions the games use

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and talk to Google Play.
  • Billing: to offer in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID and ad services: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in section 2.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: declared by Google libraries inside the ads SDK for short scheduled tasks. The games do not track you in the background.

7. Children

Our games are made for players aged 13 and over and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used one of our games, contact us and we will help remove any related data.

8. Keeping and deleting data

The save on your phone stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. Data collected by Google is kept under Google's own retention policies. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, but we will gladly help you with a request to Google.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to or limit its use for advertising. You can use these rights directly by changing your ad consent in the game's Privacy options, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or by contacting Google. You can also write to us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

If we add a feature that changes what data is processed (for example analytics or online leaderboards), we will update this page and the Google Play Data safety section before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
